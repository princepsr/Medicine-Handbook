# Guide for the doctor reviewing this handbook

*Thank you for reviewing. This handbook is a plain-language guide for lay readers in India. It was written by an AI from published sources and **no doctor has reviewed it yet.** It aims to help a reader understand reports and medicines and spot misleading advice. It is not meant to give treatment advice.*

## What would help most

1. **Mark anything that is wrong, unsafe or misleading** for a lay reader, even if it is only a matter of emphasis.
2. **Check the `⚠ verify` items.** These are claims the author could not confirm in a primary source. Each chapter ends with a "Not verified" box that lists them.
3. **Tell us what is missing** that a patient would be harmed by not knowing, and what is included that is not needed.
4. **Check Indian specifics:** scheme names and amounts, helplines, schedules, legal statements. These change often.

## Rules the book follows (please tell us if any is unwise)

- No drug doses anywhere.
- Reference ranges are examples; readers are told to use the range printed on their own report.
- Every chapter says a doctor has not reviewed it.
- Each claim should link to a source in the chapter's Sources list.

## Known limits

- Many primary sources (government and guideline sites) could not be opened during checking, so several figures were confirmed only through summaries. See [FACT-CHECK-LOG.md](FACT-CHECK-LOG.md).
- The scope is deliberately limited. See [How to use this book](00-start-here/02-how-to-use-this-book.md).
