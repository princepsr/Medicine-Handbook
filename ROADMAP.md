# Roadmap

Status: `draft` = written, not doctor-reviewed.

**Scope (fixed).** This book builds medical awareness. It does not try to cover every condition. The disease chapters are worked examples of the method. New disease chapters are not planned; use the look-up routine in [How to use this book](00-start-here/02-how-to-use-this-book.md) for anything else.

**What remains:** doctor review, a fact-check of the chapters changed in the scope pass, and a privacy scan before any commit.
