# Glossary

*Plain-language definitions of terms used in this handbook. Where a topic has a chapter, the chapter link follows. Ranges and facts are examples; see the chapters and their "Not verified" notes.*

## A

**ABHA** a 14-digit health ID in India's digital health system · [Worked examples](../10-practical/worked-examples.md)
**Absolute risk** the actual chance of an event; compare with relative risk · [Second opinions](../08-decisions/second-opinions-and-treatment-decisions.md)
**ACE inhibitor / ARB** blood-pressure medicine classes acting on the RAAS · [Common drug classes](../04-medicines/common-drug-classes.md)
**ACR (albumin-creatinine ratio)** a urine test for early kidney damage · [Urine tests](../03-tests/urine-tests.md)
**Acute kidney injury (AKI)** a sudden fall in kidney function that is often reversible if treated early · [Kidney stones, UTI and AKI](../02-diseases/kidney-stones-uti-and-aki.md)
**Adrenaline** a fast stress hormone; also an emergency medicine for severe allergy · [The endocrine system](../01-body/endocrine-system.md)
**Albumin** the main blood protein made by the liver · [Liver and gallbladder](../01-body/liver-and-gallbladder.md)
**Alveoli** tiny air sacs in the lungs · [Lungs and breathing](../01-body/lungs-and-breathing.md)
**Anaemia** too little haemoglobin · [Anaemia](../02-diseases/anaemia.md)
**Antibiotic** a medicine that kills or stops bacteria; does not work on viruses · [Antibiotics and resistance](../04-medicines/antibiotics-and-resistance.md)
**Antibody** a protein made by the immune system to lock onto a germ · [The immune system](../01-body/immune-system.md)
**Antigen** a part of a germ that the immune system recognises
**Ascites** fluid collecting in the belly, often from cirrhosis · [Liver, fatty liver and hepatitis](../02-diseases/liver.md)
**Atherosclerosis** plaque narrowing the arteries · [Coronary heart disease](../02-diseases/coronary-heart-disease.md)
**Atrial fibrillation** an irregular heartbeat that raises stroke risk · [Stroke](../02-diseases/stroke.md)
**Autoimmune** the immune system attacking the body's own tissue

## B

**Bacteria** single-celled germs; some cause infection · [Antibiotics and resistance](../04-medicines/antibiotics-and-resistance.md)
**Beta cell** a pancreas cell that makes insulin · [The endocrine system](../01-body/endocrine-system.md)
**Bile** a liver fluid that helps digest fat · [Liver and gallbladder](../01-body/liver-and-gallbladder.md)
**Bilirubin** a yellow waste pigment from old red cells · [Liver and kidney function tests](../03-tests/liver-and-kidney-tests.md)
**Biopsy** a tissue sample examined under a microscope · [Cells, genes and how cancer starts](../01-body/cells-and-cancer-basics.md)
**BMI (body mass index)** weight in kg divided by height in metres squared · [Obesity and metabolic syndrome](../02-diseases/obesity-and-metabolic-syndrome.md)
**Bone marrow** the soft tissue in bones that makes blood cells · [Blood](../01-body/blood.md)
**Booster** an extra vaccine dose that renews protection · [Vaccines and immunisation](../07-prevention/vaccines-and-immunisation.md)

## C

**Calorie (kcal)** a unit of food energy · [Nutrition and the Indian diet](../05-nutrition/nutrition-and-the-indian-diet.md)
**Cancer** uncontrolled, invasive cell growth · [Cells, genes and how cancer starts](../01-body/cells-and-cancer-basics.md)
**Capillary** the smallest blood vessel, where oxygen and nutrients cross into tissue
**CBC (complete blood count)** a blood test counting red cells, white cells and platelets · [CBC](../03-tests/cbc.md)
**CDSCO** India's central drug regulator · [Fixed-dose combinations and spurious drugs](../04-medicines/fdc-and-spurious-drugs.md)
**Cholesterol** a waxy fat needed for cells and hormones · [Metabolism and cholesterol](../01-body/hormones-metabolism-homeostasis.md)
**Chronic** lasting a long time
**CKD (chronic kidney disease)** long-term kidney damage or low filtering · [Chronic kidney disease](../02-diseases/chronic-kidney-disease.md)
**Cirrhosis** severe liver scarring · [Liver, fatty liver and hepatitis](../02-diseases/liver.md)
**Clotting** the process of blood forming a plug and a mesh to stop bleeding · [Blood](../01-body/blood.md)
**COPD** chronic obstructive pulmonary disease · [Asthma and COPD](../02-diseases/asthma-and-copd.md)
**Creatinine** a muscle waste product used to assess the kidneys · [Liver and kidney function tests](../03-tests/liver-and-kidney-tests.md)
**CRP** C-reactive protein, an inflammation marker · [Inflammation, infection and heart markers](../03-tests/inflammation-infection-and-heart-markers.md)
**CT scan** X-ray slices of the body · [Imaging and ECG reports](../03-tests/imaging-and-ecg-reports.md)
**Culture (and sensitivity)** growing germs from a sample and testing which antibiotics work

## D

**Dementia** a group of brain conditions that cause lasting decline in memory and thinking; not normal ageing · [Ageing and elderly care](../06-life-stages/ageing-and-elderly-care.md)
**Dengue** a mosquito-borne viral fever · [Common fevers in India](../02-diseases/common-fevers-india.md)
**DEXA scan** a low-dose X-ray scan that measures bone density · [Osteoporosis and arthritis](../02-diseases/osteoporosis-and-arthritis.md)
**Diabetes** high blood sugar from lack or poor action of insulin · [Diabetes](../02-diseases/diabetes.md)
**Diagnosis** naming the disease
**Dialysis** a treatment that cleans the blood when the kidneys fail · [Chronic kidney disease](../02-diseases/chronic-kidney-disease.md)
**DPCO / NPPA** the Drugs (Prices Control) Order / the price regulator · [Generic, brand and prices](../04-medicines/generic-brand-and-prices.md)

## E

**ECG** electrocardiogram, a tracing of the heart's electrical activity · [Imaging and ECG reports](../03-tests/imaging-and-ecg-reports.md)
**Echo** echocardiogram, an ultrasound of the heart
**Ejection fraction** the share of blood the heart pumps out with each beat
**eGFR** estimated glomerular filtration rate, a kidney filtering estimate · [Kidneys](../01-body/kidneys.md)
**Electrolytes** body salts such as sodium and potassium · [Metabolism and electrolytes](../01-body/hormones-metabolism-homeostasis.md)
**Endoscopy** a camera test of the gut · [Common digestive diseases](../02-diseases/common-digestive-diseases.md)
**Enzyme** a protein that speeds up a chemical reaction
**Epilepsy** a condition with repeated seizures caused by sudden bursts of electrical activity in the brain · [The brain and nervous system](../01-body/brain-and-nervous-system.md)

## F

**Familial hypercholesterolaemia** inherited very high LDL cholesterol that starts early in life · [Lipid profile](../03-tests/lipid-profile.md)
**Ferritin** a protein that stores iron; the blood level reflects iron stores · [Vitamin and iron tests](../03-tests/vitamins-and-iron-studies.md)
**Fibrosis** scarring · [Liver, fatty liver and hepatitis](../02-diseases/liver.md)
**Fixed-dose combination (FDC)** several medicines in one tablet · [Fixed-dose combinations and spurious drugs](../04-medicines/fdc-and-spurious-drugs.md)

## G

**Generic** a medicine named by its active ingredient · [Generic, brand and prices](../04-medicines/generic-brand-and-prices.md)
**GERD** acid reflux disease · [Common digestive diseases](../02-diseases/common-digestive-diseases.md)
**Glucose** blood sugar, the body's main fuel
**Goitre** a swollen thyroid · [Thyroid](../02-diseases/thyroid.md)
**Gout** joint attacks caused by uric acid crystals · [Osteoporosis and arthritis](../02-diseases/osteoporosis-and-arthritis.md)

## H

**Haemoglobin** the oxygen-carrying protein in red cells · [Blood](../01-body/blood.md)
**HbA1c** a 2 to 3 month average blood-sugar marker · [Diabetes](../02-diseases/diabetes.md)
**HDL / LDL** "good" / "bad" cholesterol carriers · [Lipid profile](../03-tests/lipid-profile.md)
**Hepatitis** inflammation of the liver, often from a virus · [Liver, fatty liver and hepatitis](../02-diseases/liver.md)
**Herd immunity** community protection when most people are immune · [Vaccines and immunisation](../07-prevention/vaccines-and-immunisation.md)
**Hormone** a chemical messenger in the blood · [The endocrine system](../01-body/endocrine-system.md)
**Hypertension** persistently high blood pressure · [High blood pressure](../02-diseases/high-blood-pressure.md)
**Hypothyroidism / hyperthyroidism** an underactive / overactive thyroid · [Thyroid](../02-diseases/thyroid.md)

## I

**Immunity** the body's defence against germs · [The immune system](../01-body/immune-system.md)
**Inflammation** the body's response to injury or infection
**Insulin** the hormone that lets glucose into cells · [The endocrine system](../01-body/endocrine-system.md)
**Insulin resistance** cells respond poorly to insulin · [Obesity and metabolic syndrome](../02-diseases/obesity-and-metabolic-syndrome.md)
**INR** a standardised blood clotting time · [Blood](../01-body/blood.md)

## J–K

**Jaundice** yellow eyes and skin from bilirubin · [Liver and gallbladder](../01-body/liver-and-gallbladder.md)
**Ketones** fuel molecules made from fat; high levels signal a danger in diabetes

## L

**LFT / KFT** liver / kidney function tests · [Liver and kidney function tests](../03-tests/liver-and-kidney-tests.md)
**Lipid profile** the cholesterol and blood-fat panel · [Lipid profile](../03-tests/lipid-profile.md)
**Living will** a written instruction about the medical treatment you do or do not want if you cannot speak for yourself · [Ageing and elderly care](../06-life-stages/ageing-and-elderly-care.md)
**Lp(a) (lipoprotein a)** an inherited cholesterol particle that raises heart risk; often tested once in a lifetime · [Lipid profile](../03-tests/lipid-profile.md)

## M

**Malaria** a mosquito-borne parasite fever · [Common fevers in India](../02-diseases/common-fevers-india.md)
**MASLD / NAFLD** fatty liver disease (new / old name) · [Liver, fatty liver and hepatitis](../02-diseases/liver.md)
**Medication-overuse headache** a headache made worse by taking painkillers too often · [The brain and nervous system](../01-body/brain-and-nervous-system.md)
**Metabolic syndrome** a cluster of belly obesity, high sugar, high blood pressure and abnormal blood fats · [Obesity and metabolic syndrome](../02-diseases/obesity-and-metabolic-syndrome.md)
**Metabolism** all the body's chemical reactions · [Metabolism](../01-body/hormones-metabolism-homeostasis.md)
**Migraine** a recurring headache, often one-sided and throbbing, sometimes with nausea or light sensitivity · [The brain and nervous system](../01-body/brain-and-nervous-system.md)
**MRI** a scan using a magnet and radio waves · [Imaging and ECG reports](../03-tests/imaging-and-ecg-reports.md)
**MRP** maximum retail price printed on a pack · [Generic, brand and prices](../04-medicines/generic-brand-and-prices.md)

## N

**NABL** the national board that accredits (checks the quality of) laboratories · [Choosing a lab](../03-tests/choosing-a-lab-and-spotting-wrong-reports.md)
**Nephron** the kidney's filter unit · [Kidneys](../01-body/kidneys.md)
**NLEM** National List of Essential Medicines · [Generic, brand and prices](../04-medicines/generic-brand-and-prices.md)
**NSAID** a painkiller class (ibuprofen, diclofenac) that can harm the stomach and kidneys · [Painkillers, steroids, acidity pills and supplements](../04-medicines/painkillers-steroids-acidity-supplements.md)
**NSQ** not of standard quality (a medicine) · [Fixed-dose combinations and spurious drugs](../04-medicines/fdc-and-spurious-drugs.md)

## O–P

**Overdiagnosis** finding a disease that would never have caused harm · [Second opinions](../08-decisions/second-opinions-and-treatment-decisions.md)
**Palliative care** care that relieves pain and other symptoms and supports the family, alongside treatment · [Common cancers in India](../02-diseases/common-cancers-in-india.md)
**Pancreas** the organ that makes insulin and digestive juices · [The endocrine system](../01-body/endocrine-system.md)
**PCOS** polycystic ovary syndrome · [PCOS and women's health](../02-diseases/pcos-and-womens-health.md)
**Platelet** a clotting cell fragment · [Blood](../01-body/blood.md)
**Pneumonia** an infection of the lung that fills the air sacs with fluid · [Asthma, COPD and chest infections](../02-diseases/asthma-and-copd.md)
**Polypharmacy** taking many medicines at once, which raises the risk of side effects and interactions · [Ageing and elderly care](../06-life-stages/ageing-and-elderly-care.md)
**PPI** proton pump inhibitor, an acid-reducing medicine · [Painkillers, steroids, acidity pills and supplements](../04-medicines/painkillers-steroids-acidity-supplements.md)
**Prediabetes** blood sugar above normal but below diabetes · [Diabetes](../02-diseases/diabetes.md)
**Pre-eclampsia** high blood pressure in pregnancy · [PCOS and women's health](../02-diseases/pcos-and-womens-health.md)
**PSA (prostate-specific antigen)** a blood protein made by the prostate; it can be raised by benign causes and is not a cancer test on its own · [Screening and check-ups](../07-prevention/screening-and-checkups.md)

## R

**RAAS** the renin-angiotensin-aldosterone blood-pressure system · [Kidneys](../01-body/kidneys.md)
**Receptor** a lock on a cell that a hormone or medicine fits
**Reference range** the printed "normal" band on a lab report · [How lab reports work](../03-tests/01-how-lab-reports-work.md)
**Relative risk** the percentage change in risk · [Second opinions](../08-decisions/second-opinions-and-treatment-decisions.md)
**Resistance (antibiotic)** bacteria that no longer respond to a medicine · [Antibiotics and resistance](../04-medicines/antibiotics-and-resistance.md)

## S

**Screening** testing people without symptoms · [Screening and health check-ups](../07-prevention/screening-and-checkups.md)
**Seizure** a sudden burst of abnormal electrical activity in the brain that can cause shaking or loss of awareness · [The brain and nervous system](../01-body/brain-and-nervous-system.md)
**Side effect** an unwanted effect of a medicine · [How medicines work](../04-medicines/how-medicines-work.md)
**Statin** a cholesterol-lowering medicine · [Lipid profile](../03-tests/lipid-profile.md)
**Stroke** loss of blood supply to part of the brain · [Stroke](../02-diseases/stroke.md)
**Subclinical** a test is abnormal but there are few symptoms · [Thyroid](../02-diseases/thyroid.md)

## T–U

**TB (tuberculosis)** a bacterial infection, mostly of the lungs · [Tuberculosis](../02-diseases/tuberculosis.md)
**Thalassaemia** an inherited haemoglobin disorder · [Blood](../01-body/blood.md)
**Thyroid** a neck gland that sets body speed · [Thyroid](../02-diseases/thyroid.md)
**Triglycerides** blood fat · [Lipid profile](../03-tests/lipid-profile.md)
**T-score** a bone density result compared with a healthy young adult; -2.5 or lower defines osteoporosis · [Osteoporosis and arthritis](../02-diseases/osteoporosis-and-arthritis.md)
**TSH** thyroid-stimulating hormone, the brain's signal to the thyroid · [Thyroid](../02-diseases/thyroid.md)
**Ultrasound (USG)** a scan using sound waves · [Imaging and ECG reports](../03-tests/imaging-and-ecg-reports.md)

## V–Z

**Vaccine** a safe lesson for the immune system · [Vaccines and immunisation](../07-prevention/vaccines-and-immunisation.md)
**Varices** swollen veins, for example in the food pipe, that can bleed in cirrhosis · [Liver, fatty liver and hepatitis](../02-diseases/liver.md)
**Virus** a tiny germ that needs a host cell; antibiotics do not work · [Antibiotics and resistance](../04-medicines/antibiotics-and-resistance.md)
**Visceral fat** fat inside the belly · [Obesity and metabolic syndrome](../02-diseases/obesity-and-metabolic-syndrome.md)
**Vitamin B12 / D** vitamins for nerves and blood / bones · [Vitamin and iron tests](../03-tests/vitamins-and-iron-studies.md)
**White blood cell** a defence cell · [Blood](../01-body/blood.md)
